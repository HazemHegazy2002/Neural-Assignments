     • Fleiss' κ is lower because keyphrase extraction
       is an open-ended task: annotators select semantically
       equivalent but lexically different phrases, which
       binary Kappa penalizes heavily.
     • Pairwise F1 is the standard primary IAA measure for
       this task type (see Hasan & Ng 2014; Kim et al. 2010).
     • Std Dev > 0 across paragraphs confirms genuine
       per-paragraph variation in annotator agreement.
